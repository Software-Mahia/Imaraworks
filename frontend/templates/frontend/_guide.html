Guide

From invoice to paid, in three moves.

One request, one approval chain, one payment. Every step is appended to the audit trail.

1

Raise the request

Choose the vendor, project and amount, then submit it for approval.

2

Collect sign-off

A manager approves, and Finance adds a second approval when the amount needs one.

3

Finance pays

Once every required level signs off, Finance sends the payment. A declined attempt can be retried.

Two approval tiers

A manager always approves. Above the limit, Finance approves too — in either order — and Finance pays both routes.
